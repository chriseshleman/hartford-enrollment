# Corrections

Every change that alters a number on hartfordschoolenrollment.us gets logged here:
what changed, when, and who flagged it. Nothing is quietly edited.

Spotted a problem? Email <feedback@hartfordschoolenrollment.us> or
[open an issue](https://github.com/chriseshleman/hartford-enrollment/issues).

The reconstructed years, 2002-03 to 2015-16, are the likeliest place for errors.
Better figures from the *Sheff v. O'Neill* compliance filings would be especially welcome.

---

## Format

Newest first. One entry per change.

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — short description
**Reported by:** name, or "internal review"
**What was wrong:** the previous figure or claim
**What it is now:** the corrected figure or claim
**Why:** the source or reasoning behind the change
**Affects:** which chart, which years
```

---

## Open questions

Known problems not yet resolved. Listed so they aren't mistaken for settled.

- **2002-03 baseline.** The Sheff record reports both 10% and 17% for the share of
  Hartford minority students in reduced-isolation settings. The lower figure is used.
  The two may measure different populations; the primary stipulation would resolve it.
- **2006-07.** Reported as both 9% and 11%. The lower figure is used, which produces a
  small reversal against 2005-06.
- **1986-2001 magnets and choice.** Treated as effectively zero, supported by federal
  data showing Hartford magnet enrollment at zero for 1999-2001. If any child was
  enrolled in an interdistrict program before then, it is not captured here.
- **Vo-tech before 2016.** Scaled from a single school, A. I. Prince Technical.
  Hartford residents at other technical high schools are absorbed into that scaling
  factor rather than counted.
